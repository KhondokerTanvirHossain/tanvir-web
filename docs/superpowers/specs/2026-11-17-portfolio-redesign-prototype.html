How I lead

Four habits, learned the expensive way.

Ownership over scope

If the bank's servers are down and the deployment is mine, I am at the bank. Titles describe the job; the gap between teams is also the job.

A definition of done that means something

On Better Health, done means a real doctor completes a prescription unassisted on production. Not merged, not deployed: used.

Tests first, then code

Payment flows and prescriptions do not get a second chance. The failing test comes first, and the suite stays fast enough that nobody skips it.

Say the uncomfortable thing early

A slipped date, a wrong assumption, a security gap. I report it the day I find it, with what I will do about it.
